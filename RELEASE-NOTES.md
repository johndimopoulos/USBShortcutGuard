# USBShortcutGuard 3.0

- Checks USB devices at startup and when connected.
- Quarantines files matching strong shortcut-virus signatures and restores hidden originals.
- Greek and English interface and instructions.
- Administrator-only quarantine viewer with export and permanent deletion.
- Progress window for scans lasting longer than five seconds.
- Cleanup notifications from both the tray and Start menu.
- Improved quarantine viewer layout for enlarged text and display scaling.

Download USBShortcutGuard-Setup.exe and verify SHA256SUMS.txt.

Reinstall/update preserves logs and quarantine. Complete reinstall and uninstall delete them.
